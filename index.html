03 — The Method

A closed loop between the lab and the timeline.

Step 01

Screen

Audiences view the cut in a controlled lab or at-home panel.

Step 02

Capture

EEG, HRV, GSR and eye-tracking recorded synchronously to timecode.

Step 03

Model

Signals mapped to an emotional timeline, scene by scene.

Step 04

Refine

Post-production adjusts edit, colour and sound against the data.

Step 05

Validate

Re-test the recut to confirm measurable emotional lift.

Step 06

Deliver

Final master + full neurometric report and recommendations.

Manifesto
01

Emotion is measurable.

The human nervous system responds to a cut, a colour, a chord before the conscious mind forms an opinion. We record those signals and treat them as ground truth.

02

The edit is a hypothesis.

Every version of a film is a testable claim about how it will make people feel. We run the experiment, read the data, and refine — replacing guesswork with evidence.

03

Craft has the final cut.

Data illuminates; it never dictates. Our colourists, editors and sound designers interpret the neurometrics through taste and intention. The art always leads.
